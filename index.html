03 · A little context

About me

I study computer science at VIT Vellore. I grew up in Bengaluru, started out building AI systems, and now spend most of my time somewhere between code, cognition, and hardware.

What ties my work together is a preference for things you can inspect and test. In AI that meant compilers and explicit memory. In robotics it means measuring the path from a sensor event to a physical response.

I like ambitious questions, learning outside my field, and making rough ideas concrete enough to be wrong. Eventually, I want to help define a field rather than simply work inside one.
